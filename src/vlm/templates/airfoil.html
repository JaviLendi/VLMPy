NACA Airfoil

Define the profile and flight condition for the interactive preview.

Profile

NACA definition

infoNACA Definition Info

  • NACA Profile: Four-digit NACA code (e.g. 2412). Each digit has a slider below, and the text field and sliders stay in sync.
  • Camber: First digit. Maximum camber as a percentage of the chord.
  • Camber position: Second digit. Chordwise position of the maximum camber, in tenths of the chord.
  • Thickness / Thickness detail: Third and fourth digits. Together they give the maximum thickness as a percentage of the chord.
Condition

Flight and geometry

infoFlight and Geometry Info

  • Free Stream Velocity: Undisturbed flow speed in meters per second (m/s).
  • Angle of Attack: Angle between the chord line and the free stream, in degrees.
  • Chord Length: Airfoil chord in meters (m). Scales the coordinates.
  • Number of Points: Points used to discretize the airfoil contour. More points give a smoother profile.
Analysis

2D panel method

info2D Panel Method Info

  • Formulation: Singularity distribution used on the panels: sources plus vortex (Hess-Smith) or doublets with a wake.
  • Number of panels: Panels used to discretize the contour (20-400). More panels increase resolution and computing time.
  • Solve panel method: Runs the solver with the current profile and flight condition, and enables the result views next to the preview.

The model assumes incompressible, inviscid, irrotational flow, so it does not predict viscous drag or stall.

Data

Save and load

infoSave and Load Info

  • Save airfoil coordinates (CSV): Downloads the airfoil coordinates as a CSV file.
  • Save: Stores the current airfoil configuration with a name and description.
  • Load: Opens the list of saved airfoil configurations to restore one.
Live preview

Interactive NACA Airfoil

infoPreview Info

  • Preview: Airfoil shape with upper surface, lower surface and camber line.
  • Cp: Pressure coefficient distribution along the chord (after solving).
  • CL(x): Lift coefficient contribution along the chord (after solving).
  • Panels: Panel discretization used by the solver.
  • Flow field: Streamlines and/or Cp map around the airfoil, with zoom on the airfoil or the full domain.

The result tabs appear only after running the panel method.
